# SILENT BLADE v0.4.1 — combat expansion

This is an incremental update to the existing Godot 4.3 v0.4 project. It does not replace the game, input scheme, scene, campaign data or block/parry system.

## Katana combo

The player has a three-strike sequence. Each strike has its own damage, active hitbox duration, cooldown, hitbox dimensions/offset, knockback impulse and visual style. The sequence advances after each accepted strike and returns to strike 1 when the 0.85-second link window expires.

| Strike | Damage | Hitbox active | Cooldown | Hitbox size | Knockback |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Quick Slash | 22 | 0.12 s | 0.27 s | 74 × 34 | 155 |
| Cross Cut | 31 | 0.16 s | 0.32 s | 88 × 44 | 245 |
| Heavy Finisher | 48 | 0.23 s | 0.50 s | 112 × 56 | 430 |

Each strike changes the shared hitbox's dimensions and position. The active visual differs: narrow diagonal slash, two crossing streaks, then a wide red arc with a bright impact flare. A successful parry's counter window still modifies the damage of the next strike by +50% without changing the combo's identity.

## Enemy types

The level spawns one live instance of each enemy profile through `_spawn_enemy()` in `scripts/main.gd`. The profile is applied before combat starts and changes actual AI/combat values, collision size and drawn silhouette.

| Type | Health | Movement speed | Attack damage | Attack cooldown | Telegraph | Active | Recovery | Knockback resistance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Guard | 65 | 82 | 18 | 1.20 s | 0.58 s | 0.18 s | 0.82 s | 1.00× |
| Scout | 42 | 145 | 12 | 0.72 s | 0.32 s | 0.13 s | 0.48 s | 0.55× |
| Heavy Guard | 145 | 48 | 32 | 1.50 s | 0.90 s | 0.24 s | 1.15 s | 2.25× |

The Scout is fast and fragile, the Guard is balanced, and the Heavy Guard is slower, tougher, hits harder and resists displacement. All three use the same telegraph → execution → recovery state machine, with timings supplied by their profile.

## Damage, knockback and death

The player validates the physics-area target and calls the enemy's dedicated `receive_damage(amount, source_position, knockback_force)` method. Enemy profile damage is read through `get_attack_damage()` and is the actual damage applied to the player; a guard break transmits 67% of that enemy-specific damage. That method applies damage, divides the impulse by the target's knockback resistance, sets horizontal velocity, adds a brief vertical lift and moves the enemy into a knockback state. The three strike profiles therefore produce different physical impulses, and the three enemy profiles respond differently to the same impulse.

Death is guarded by `is_dead`, clamps health to zero, disables collision, emits one `died(enemy_type)` signal and calls `queue_free()`. The level increments its defeated-enemy count from that signal, rather than polling the lifetime of one hard-coded Guard reference.

## Existing v0.4 mechanics preserved

- Hold-to-block and release-to-stop behavior.
- 0.18-second parry window, parry interrupt, counter window and +50% next-strike damage.
- Finite guard stamina, guard break and stamina regeneration.
- Enemy preparation/telegraph, active attack and recovery phases.
- Android landscape setup, virtual joystick, touch action buttons and focus/cancel input release.
- Existing mission start, pause, result and replay flows.

## Verification record

Automated checks are structural contract tests over the source, not simulated Godot gameplay. In this environment, `pytest -q` reports **18 passed**. The test suite checks all three data profiles and their distinct stats, level instantiation and signal wiring, all three combo strike profiles and their different parameters, hitbox/visual selection, knockback resistance application and one-shot death handling. Existing v0.4 block/parry and enemy attack contract checks remain in the suite.

Godot 4.x/4.3 is not installed in the current build environment. Therefore GDScript import/parser validation, a headless scene launch, interactive combat playtesting and Android build/device testing were **not run**. `.github/workflows/godot-validation.yml` remains available to perform the Godot 4.3 import and headless smoke checks in GitHub Actions after the project is pushed.
